# voices-unboxed-climate

Materials from the 2026 Voices Unboxed (Climate Edition) series of workshops as part of the Climatebase fellowship.

---

Voices Unboxed is a practice-based communication space for climate and technical professionals who want to communicate their work with greater clarity, confidence, and impact.

We believe that some of the most important ideas (in climate, energy, and other complex fields) don’t always land, not because they lack value, but because they are difficult to explain in a way that connects.

Voices Unboxed was created to close that gap.

Through interactive sessions, workshops, and real-world practice, we help professionals:

- translate complex ideas into clear, human language
- move beyond technical explanations to meaningful conversations
- respond to questions, pushback, and misinformation with confidence
- develop their own authentic communication style

This is not presentation training. It’s a space to practise real conversations: the ones that happen in meetings, interviews, and everyday interactions.

We started in the climate space, but the challenge is broader: in any technical field, if an idea is not understood, it cannot scale.

Voices Unboxed exists to make sure it does.
